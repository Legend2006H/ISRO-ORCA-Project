# ORCA: Marine Ecosystem Reasoning with Collaborative Agents
**SIH 2026 • Problem Statement: SIH26176 • ISRO**

## Project Motivation & Why We Chose This Topic
The marine ecosystem is critical for livelihoods, food security, biodiversity, and coastal resilience. Every day, vast volumes of satellite Earth Observation and oceanographic data (SST, chlorophyll, weather) are generated by agencies like ISRO and INCOIS. However, accessing and interpreting this data is complex for end-users like fishermen and coastal authorities.

We chose this topic to bridge the gap between complex marine datasets and the people who need them most. By building an Agentic AI-powered conversational platform, we can help marine stakeholders interact naturally with marine information, ensuring they can easily find the safest fishing zones, optimize routes, and receive life-saving weather hazard alerts.

## What We Want To Do (The Goal)
Our primary goal is to build a production-minded **Marine Decision-Support System (ORCA)**. Instead of a generic chatbot, ORCA is an intelligent multi-agent orchestrator. 

When a user asks a natural-language marine question (e.g., *"Where is the nearest Potential Fishing Zone to me today?"*), ORCA will:
1. **Understand Intent:** Detect the language (English, Hindi, Marathi) and decompose the request.
2. **Retrieve Data:** Autonomously fetch authoritative marine data (INCOIS, MOSDAC, Weather, Bathymetry).
3. **Execute Deterministic Reasoning:** Calculate distances, check geofences, and evaluate safety risks using traditional GIS and spatial computations (no LLM hallucination).
4. **Synthesize & Explain:** Rank the candidates and use an LLM to generate a natural, conversational response backed entirely by the retrieved evidence.
5. **Visualize:** Present the answer alongside an interactive WebGIS map, evidence ledger, and real-time alerts.

## Core Strategy
- **Never let the LLM invent facts:** The AI only plans, translates, and explains. All spatial, temporal, and risk calculations are handled by deterministic Python/GIS services.
- **Evidence-Backed:** Every recommendation is tied to a specific evidence record (source, timestamp, value) to ensure complete transparency and zero-hallucination.
- **Multilingual Support:** Fully supports contextual conversations in Indian regional languages.
- **Safety First:** Prioritizes hazard alerts (cyclones, high waves) and boundary geofencing to protect fishermen.
