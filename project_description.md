# ORCA: Marine Ecosystem Reasoning with Collaborative Agents
**SIH 2026 • Problem Statement: SIH26176 • ISRO**

## Project Motivation & Why We Chose This Topic
The marine ecosystem is critical for livelihoods, food security, biodiversity, and coastal resilience. Every day, vast volumes of satellite Earth Observation and oceanographic data (SST, chlorophyll, weather) are generated by agencies like ISRO and INCOIS. However, accessing and interpreting this data is complex for end-users like fishermen and coastal authorities.

We chose this topic to bridge the gap between complex marine datasets and the people who need them most. By building an evidence-grounded decision-support conversational platform, we can help marine stakeholders interact naturally with marine information, ensuring they can easily find favorable fishing zones, evaluate navigation routes, and receive timely weather hazard alerts.

## What We Want To Do (The Goal)
Our primary goal is to build a production-minded **Marine Decision-Support System (ORCA)**. Instead of an unconstrained chatbot, ORCA combines deterministic spatial GIS logic with grounded language synthesis. 

When a user asks a natural-language marine question (e.g., *"Where is the nearest Potential Fishing Zone to me today?"*), ORCA will:
1. **Understand Intent:** Detect the language (English, Hindi, Marathi) and extract location entities.
2. **Retrieve Data:** Fetch authoritative marine data (INCOIS, MOSDAC, Weather, Bathymetry) or validated local caches.
3. **Execute Deterministic Reasoning:** Calculate distances, check geofences, and evaluate safety risks against configured vessel thresholds using traditional GIS algorithms.
4. **Synthesize & Explain:** Rank candidates and use an LLM to generate a natural, conversational response backed entirely by the retrieved evidence.
5. **Visualize:** Present the answer alongside an interactive WebGIS map, evidence ledger, and ocean state alerts.

## Core Strategy
- **Evidence-Grounded Synthesis:** The LLM strictly summarizes and explains verified operational context. All spatial, temporal, and risk calculations are handled by deterministic Python/GIS services.
- **Evidence-Backed:** Every recommendation is tied to a specific evidence record (source, timestamp, value) to ensure complete transparency and traceability.
- **Multilingual Support:** Fully supports contextual conversations in Indian regional languages.
- **Safety-Centric Decision Support:** Prioritizes hazard alerts (high waves, wind) and boundary geofencing to inform fishermen before departure.
